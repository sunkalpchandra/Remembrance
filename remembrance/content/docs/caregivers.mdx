## Becoming a caregiver

Pick **Caregiver** during onboarding. Before the dashboard unlocks you
review and accept a HIPAA acknowledgment — the acceptance timestamp is
recorded on your account.

## Managing patients

From **Dashboard → Patients** you can:

- **Create patient accounts.** The account is created in Clerk with the
  patient role and linked to you. Patients sign in with the credentials
  you hand them and go straight to chat — onboarding is pre-completed.
- **Edit profiles** — name, age, family members, familiar places, and
  notes that shape how the companion talks with them.
- **Write notes.** Notes are stored for you and also pushed into the
  patient's memory store, so the companion can gently reference them in
  conversation. The patient gets an in-app notification.
- **View memory graphs** per patient under **Dashboard → Memories**.

## Activity feed

The dashboard home shows week-over-week engagement: conversations
started, memories created, notes added — each event attributed to the
patient who triggered it. The full filterable log lives under
**Dashboard → Activity**.

## Data access

Caregiver access is scoped: every query checks that the patient row is
linked to your caregiver id before returning anything. Deleting a
patient account removes their Clerk user, database rows, and Mem0
memories.
